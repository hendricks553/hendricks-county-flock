Send yours

Received records from any Indiana agency, on any vendor? Send them to hendricks553@proton.me. They get posted here and passed to Eyes Off Indiana's public archive.

Documents are published as received. Personal information about individual officials is redacted before posting. Denials are worth sending too, since a denial letter is a record of its own.
