Unexamined

Pittsboro and Lizton no page yet

Neither town publishes a transparency portal, and nobody has filed a records request with either yet. What is known comes secondhand: both appear on Plainfield's published sharing lists, which puts their cameras inside the same national network as everyone else's.

Seven cameras between two small towns sounds trivial until you look at the per-capita column. Lizton has the highest rate in the county by a wide margin.

These two get pages when somebody sends documents. The request template works for any Indiana town, and residency is not required to file.
